# 💎 PISO Chain Ecosystem Developer Grants & Hackathon Program

Welcome to the **PISO Chain Ecosystem Grants Program**. We provide non-dilutive funding, technical mentorship, and marketing support to developers, researchers, and AI agent engineers building the future of post-quantum Web3 infrastructure.

---

## 🎯 Target Focus Areas

1. **Autonomous AI Agent OS Infrastructure**:
   - Integration with CrewAI, LangChain, AutoGen, LlamaIndex, and OpenWebUI.
   - Micro-payment rails and task escrow runners on PISO Chain.

2. **Post-Quantum Cryptography & Zero-Knowledge Privacy**:
   - Hybrid NIST FIPS 204 ML-DSA and SPHINCS+ signing libraries.
   - ZK Merkle Proof guardian social recovery tools (`PISOZKRecovery.sol`).

3. **EVM DeFi & Account Abstraction Tooling**:
   - Native EIP-4337 Paymasters, UserOperation bundlers, and custom smart contract wallets.
   - Cross-chain asset bridges (`PISOBridge.sol`).

## 📝 Application & Review Process

1. **Submit Grant Application**:
   - Fork this repository and create a proposal in `grants/proposals/your_project.md` outlining project scope, team background, milestone deliverables, and budget requested.
2. **Technical Review**:
   - The PISO Chain Core Technical Committee will review your proposal within 5 business days.
3. **Milestone Distribution**:
   - Grant funds ($PISO / USDT) are disbursed in tranches upon completed milestone verification on PISO Chain Mainnet (`Chain ID: 2026001`).
